WITNESSETH

That the “Water Supplier” and the “Concessionaire”, in consideration of the Mutual Covenants and Agreements made hereby these presents, enter into Contract of Water Service effective {{ contract_date_display }} (date) with the following conditions:

A. INSTALLATION

  1. The Water Supplier shall install a water service connection to the property of the concessionaire as described in the accomplished application for installation form. Installation will be made only upon approval of the submitted application form by the General Manager and upon payment of the required service application fee and other charges relative thereto.
  2. The water meter should be installed only at the designated water service pads/tapping points by the water supplier authorized plumbers. All materials after the water meter will be provided by the concessionaire; labor expenses relative thereto will be at the concessionaire’s expense.
    1. Distribution lines after the water meter shall be maintained by the concessionaire and any wastage or damage or leakage not caused by the District shall be shouldered by the concessionaire.
    2. For concessionaire’s whose service line passes through private property/ies, it is the concessionaire’s responsibility to secure written permission from the rightful owner of said property/ies. This written permission shall be part of the requirements for application.
  3. The water district shall have a regular inspection for maintenance or repairs of the water meter main lines, lateral lines, and service lines. If the concessionaire wishes to have house extensions for veranda, facades, garage, fences and other structure that will obstruct or interfere the district’s operation and maintenance to its water lines and facilities, the water supplier shall not be held liable for any damage on the extension done by the concessionaire which is beyond his/her lot or property.

B. DISCONNECTION

The water district shall discontinue its water services to a concessionaire in any of the following grounds:

  1. Failure to pay 3 consecutive months. Disconnection notice will be given to concessionaires after due dates. Disconnection of service connection due to delinquency will be implemented seventy-two (72) hours after receipt of disconnection notice.
  2. Any violation of RA 8041 otherwise, known as the Water Crisis Act of 1995 as provided in Rule 5, Section 3 and 5.2 or any fraudulent practice in relation to the use of the water and water system.
  3. Non-compliance to the other water supplier policies ad Board of Resolutions pertaining to water services.

C. RE-CONNECTION

  1. Disconnected installation due to delinquency and other legal ground for disconnection can be reconnected upon full payment of arrears, re-connection fees of P500.00 and penalty charges. However, reconnection maybe held pending in cases of water shortage or similar situation that will affect majority of the consumers.
  2. Request reconnection for disconnected pipelines by another person to replace the name of the original registered concessionaire may not be granted except upon application for change of name of the disconnected service connection will properly approved and upon settlement of all previous accounts and all other charges of the said registered concessionaire.
  3. Concessionaires who request voluntary disconnection of their service lines are required to pay the prevailing reconnection fee before reconnection is implemented.
  4. After payment of the service connection fee, the water meter is a property of the concessionaire, hence the concessionaire should protect it from damages and will be required to replace and buy it to the water supplier the equivalent amount in case of its loss or damage while in possession.

E. GENERAL POLICIES

  1. Payment of bills shall be made only to the water supplier designated teller’s cashiers.
  2. All unpaid bills after due shall be considered as arrears and shall be subjected to 10% penalty charge.
  3. The water supplier shall supply a water meter to the concessionaire and in turn the concessionaire should protect it from any damage and shall be required to pay the equivalent amount in case of its loss or damage while in possession.
  4. Should an issued water meter be destroyed or becomes unserviceable for any reason or another, concessionaire should immediately report it to the water supplier for appropriate action. In case of uncertainty of consumption, the average of the highest three (3) months previous consumption shall be the basis of subsequent bill consumption.
  5. The water meter shall have a warranty against factory defect of 12 months from the date of installation.
  6. Authorized representative of the water supplier shall be given by the concessionaire full access to his premises or property for the purpose of inspection, repairs and disconnection and that no one is allowed to remove or tamper any installation therein unless authorized by the District.
  7. All other policies, rules and regulations that may hereinafter be formulated by the water supplier management shall form part of this policy upon proper public notification.
  8. Should this concessionaire fail to comply with any agreement and policy, the District may terminate this contract with prior notification. Termination of contract will mean discontinuance of water service to concerned concessionaire.
