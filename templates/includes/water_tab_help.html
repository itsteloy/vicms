User guide

For first-time staff. Follow the monthly cycle, then use the click-by-click steps below.

Water Billing — beginner user manual

VERSATEC Water Billing Dashboard

1. What this dashboard is for

Each month you do four jobs, in this order: register the household (if they are new), record the meter, generate the bill, then collect payment. Do not skip a step. You cannot record a payment until a bill exists.

  1. Open Water Billing Use the left menu. Start on Overview.
  2. Customers Save the account if it is new.
  3. Readings & Billing Save the meter, then click Generate Bill.
  4. Payments Record cash or GCash against that bill.

2. How to move around

  1. The green menu on the left opens each section. Click once. Wait for “Loading…” if you see it.
  2. A green bar at the top means the save worked. A red bar means something is missing or wrong. Read it before you try again.
  3. Long lists show 50 rows at a time. Use Prev / Next under the table.
  4. Overview numbers (customers, outstanding, bills) are a snapshot. Collections on Overview and Payments can be viewed by week, month, or year, split into Cash, GCash, and total. You do not type on Overview.
  5. Use Logout at the top right when you are done.

3. Transaction: register a customer

Open Customers. Fill Register customer at the top, then click Save customer.

  1. Last name, First name, and Meter number are required. Address, contact number, email, and zone are optional.
  2. Names, service address, meter, and a new zone name save in CAPITAL LETTERS. You can type in small letters; the system converts them.
  3. Account No. is filled in for you (for example WA-2026-001). Do not invent a number.
  4. Registration Date defaults to today. Customer Type is usually Residential. Connection Status is usually Active.
  5. To add a zone, choose + Add new zone… and type the name (for example ZONE 1).
  6. Installation fee is ₱5,900. Payment: Full – ₱5,900.00, Partial – ₱3,000.00, or Custom amount…. Balance is calculated. Then click Save customer.
  7. Each real meter number can be used only once. If there is no meter yet, enter the placeholder — (em dash). Several accounts may use —.

To find someone later, type in Search (name, account, or meter). Wait a moment; the list updates from the whole database, not only the page on screen. Use Zone or Status if needed, then Filter. Click View / Edit to open the profile. The account number cannot be changed there.

Stop and ask a supervisor before you click Delete on a customer. A confirm box will appear. Deleting removes the account.

4. Transaction: record a reading and make a bill

Open Readings & Billing.

  1. Under New meter reading, choose the customer. Previous reading, unpaid balance, and installment fill in. You may correct Previous. Present is required.
  2. Consumption = Present − Previous. Current bill = consumption × ₱20. Total = current + previous unpaid + installment. Check the totals strip before you save.
  3. Reading Date defaults to the 28th of this month. Billing Period is the year-month of that date (for example 2026-08). Each customer may have only one reading per period.
  4. If the account is disconnected, a warning appears. Confirm before you save.
  5. Click Save reading. This does not print a bill yet.
  6. In History, find that row and click Generate Bill. The statement opens. Or click Generate all bills to create a bill for every reading that still has none.
  7. The due date is 15 days after the reading date.
  8. To print several statements: tick up to 6 bills, then Print selected (max 6). For one statement, click View.

Stop and ask a supervisor before you click Delete on a reading. Deleting a reading can also remove its bill.

5. Transaction: record a payment

Open Payments. The Cash / GCash / Total strip at the top uses the same week, month, or year filter as Overview. Use Record payment (not the history table) to encode.

  1. Click the Bill box and type the customer name. Pick the matching unpaid or partial bill from the list. Amount fills with the balance. You may type a smaller amount for a partial payment.
  2. Method is Cash or GCash only. For GCash, fill Reference No.
  3. Receipt No. is filled in for you (for example WP-2026-001). Payment Date defaults to today. Received By can stay as shown.
  4. Click Save payment.
  5. In Payment history, Paid means that bill has no remaining balance. Unpaid means a balance is still due.

If the Bill list says “No unpaid bills,” generate the bill on Readings & Billing first. You cannot pay before a bill exists.

Stop and ask a supervisor before you click Delete on a payment.

6. Check who still owes (Receivables)

Open Receivables to review, not to encode. Aging cards (Current, 1–30 days, and so on) show how old unpaid balances are. The table lists outstanding bills. You may tick up to 6 bills and print, same as on Readings. Use Payments if you need to record a collection.

7. Transaction: disconnect or reconnect

Open Service Status.

  1. Policy: an account with 3 months unpaid appears under For disconnection (3+ months unpaid).
  2. To schedule: pick the customer, choose Disconnection or Reconnection, set the date, then click Save action.
  3. The action stays For disconnection until someone clicks Mark disconnected or Mark reconnected in Service actions.
  4. Reconnection fee defaults to ₱500. A finalized reconnection sets the account back to Active.
  5. Water service contract: scroll to the contract panel below. Choose New for a blank form, or Reconnection / Transfer and pick a customer to prefill once. Click Save contract, then Print.

8. Reports and Audit

Open Reports. Choose a report type, then click Show. Use Export CSV for a spreadsheet file, or Print for paper. Open Audit to see a read-only trail of who saved a customer, reading, bill, or payment. You do not encode on Audit.

9. Everyday rules and mistakes

  • Do the steps in order: customer → reading → Generate Bill → payment.
  • Do not record a payment before a bill exists.
  • Do not delete a customer, reading, or payment unless a supervisor says so.
  • If a tab says “Could not load this section,” click Retry or refresh the page.
  • One reading per customer per billing period. If save fails, that period may already have a reading.
