Deductions Management

Configure deduction types and assign them to employees. Standard configs include PhilHealth Contribution, SSS Contribution, HDMF Contribution, SSS Loan, and HDMF Loan. Deductions apply on a pay run only after you assign them to employees for a pay period (with the amount per payroll). They are not applied automatically.
