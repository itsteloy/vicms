A **Water Billing Dashboard** should provide utility staff with a clear overview of customer accounts, billing, collections, consumption, and operational performance. Below are the **essential features** typically included.

## 1. Dashboard Overview

* Total Active Customers
* Total Water Connections
* Monthly Water Consumption
* Total Bills Generated
* Total Revenue Collected
* Outstanding Balance
* Collection Rate (%)
* New Customer Registrations
* Disconnected Accounts
* Reconnected Accounts

---

## 2. Customer Management

* Customer Information
* Customer Account Number
* Service Address
* Contact Information
* Customer Type (Residential, Commercial, Government, Industrial)
* Meter Number
* Connection Status (Active, Inactive, Disconnected)
* Search and Filter Customers

---

## 3. Meter Reading Management

* Record Monthly Meter Readings
* Previous Reading
* Current Reading
* Consumption Calculation
* Reading History
* Estimated Readings
* Reading Validation
* Bulk Upload Readings
* Mobile Meter Reading Integration

---

## 4. Billing Management

* Generate Monthly Bills
* Automatic Bill Computation
* Water Consumption Charges
* Fixed Charges
* Environmental Fees
* Maintenance Fees
* Taxes
* Discounts
* Penalties
* Due Dates
* Billing History
* Bill Reprinting

---

## 5. Payment Management

* Record Payments
* Partial Payments
* Full Payments
* Payment History
* Official Receipt Generation
* Payment Methods

  * Cash
  * Bank
  * Online Payment
  * E-Wallet
* Payment Confirmation

---

## 6. Accounts Receivable

* Outstanding Bills
* Aging Report
* Overdue Accounts
* Collection Tracking
* Collection Performance
* Payment Reminders

---

## 7. Reports

Generate reports such as:

* Monthly Billing Report
* Collection Report
* Outstanding Balance Report
* Consumption Report
* Customer Report
* Revenue Report
* Disconnection Report
* Reconnection Report
* Meter Reading Report
* Daily Collection Report
* Annual Revenue Report

Export options:

* PDF
* Excel
* CSV
* Print

---

## 8. Disconnection & Reconnection

* List of Accounts for Disconnection
* Disconnection Schedule
* Reconnection Requests
* Reconnection Fees
* Service Status Tracking

---

## 9. Audit Logs

* User Login History
* Billing Activities
* Payment Transactions
* Customer Record Changes
* Meter Reading Updates
* Report Generation History

---

## Recommended Navigation Menu

A practical sidebar for a water billing system includes:

1. Dashboard
2. Customers
3. Meter Readings
4. Billing
5. Payments
6. Accounts Receivable
7. Disconnection & Reconnection
8. Reports
9. Audit Logs


These features cover the core workflows of a water utility, from customer registration and meter reading through billing, payment collection, reporting, and administration. They also provide management with real-time insights into revenue, consumption, and collection performance.
